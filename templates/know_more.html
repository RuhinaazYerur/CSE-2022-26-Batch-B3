📸 Dataset Composition

  • Raw: 150 images
  • Semi-cooked: 150 images
  • Fully cooked: 150 images
  • Captured under controlled conditions
  • Balanced dataset for fair learning
The dataset was self-created due to the lack of publicly available rice cooking-stage datasets.

🧪 Training Strategy

  • Train/Validation/Test split applied
  • Data augmentation for robustness
  • Transfer learning using pre-trained CNN backbones
  • Fine-tuning top layers to improve accuracy
Fine-tuning improves sensitivity to subtle texture and moisture differences across stages.

The system preprocesses the uploaded image, extracts features using the CNN backbone, and predicts one of the three cooking states with a confidence score.

📊 Evaluation Metrics

  • Accuracy
  • Precision, Recall, F1-score
  • Confusion Matrix
  • Accuracy/Loss training curves

🧠 Common Misclassifications

  • Semi-cooked vs Fully cooked may look similar
  • Lighting variation can change texture appearance
  • New rice types may require more training data
